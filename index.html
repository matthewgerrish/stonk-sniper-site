Three rounds. Unpredictable timing.

One round per eight-hour window, at least ninety minutes apart, drawn at midnight UTC. The times stay secret until the day’s last round, so there is no window in which to buy in before a snapshot and sell after it.

Today’s commitment

—

Posted on-chain at 00:00 UTC as sha256(times ‖ salt) before any round fires.

Today’s reveal

—

Published after the third round: the three times and the salt, so anyone can recompute the hash above.

How to verify a reveal yourself

The preimage is the three execution times as unix seconds, sorted ascending and joined with commas, then a pipe, then the salt as lowercase hex: 

t1,t2,t3|saltHex

SHA-256 that exact string. It must equal the commitment posted before the day began. A skipped or degraded round is still recorded against the commitment, so a bad round cannot be quietly dropped.

Buy back. Burn. Repeat.

Burns are real buybacks paid for in SOL, from three separate sources. Each one is recorded with paired buy and burn signatures.

Base slice

5%

Of every round’s revenue, spent buying $SNIPER to burn.

Winner harvest

50%

Of any sniped coin that graduates during its hold window — sold, and the proceeds burned.

Expired dust

100%

Of unclaimed positions after they expire — swept, sold, and burned. Activates with the claim distributor.

What you are actually buying

Stonk Sniper buys the newest launches on a launchpad. That is the unfiltered intake population, where the most common outcome for any single coin is zero. The basket will often look generous in token count and thin in dollars. The filters, the winner harvest and the burn exist to push against that, and none of them change the base rate.

This is a speculative memecoin, not an investment product. Nothing here is financial advice.
